DEMO SALES AGREEMENT

Agreed will provide sales agreement software to Street Talk.

This software is under development, and Street Talk is among the first businesses to use it.

The software will be ready within one month after Street Talk pays. Street Talk’s billing period will begin once the software is ready. For example, Street Talk will receive a full 12 months of service beginning on the date the software is ready.

Agreed’s CEO will work with Street Talk’s team to set up agreements, keep terms current, and continuously improve Street Talk’s process so deals close faster while keeping contractual protections in place.

All intellectual property is owned by Agreed. All Street Talk data is owned by Street Talk.

Pricing

In the first year, Street Talk will receive a heavily discounted price of $12,000 for unlimited use.

The entire amount will be paid upfront.

In exchange for this heavily discounted price:

1. Street Talk will have each of the following roles make two introductions to sales leaders who agree to a 20-minute call with Agreed to assist with product research: .

2. Street Talk’s COO, CRO and 1 of Street Talk's sales reps each agree to participate in two 30-minute product research interviews with Agreed.

3. Street Talk gives Agreed permission to contact the lawyers who represent Street Talk’s customers and who worked on Street Talk’s agreements. Agreed may contact them only for product research. Street Talk may tell Agreed at any time not to contact the lawyers associated with any specific customer.

Security

In the first year, all software development and customer support work will be performed by Agreed’s CEO.

In the first year, Agreed’s CEO will be the only person with access to Street Talk’s data.

At any time during the first year and for one year afterward, Street Talk will be able to export its data. After that period, all Street Talk data will be deleted unless Street Talk gives Agreed written permission to retain it.

During the first year, Street Talk will use a standalone version of the software that is independent of the versions used by all other businesses. Street Talk’s data will be stored in a database that is separate from those used by all other businesses.

Agreed is responsible only for data stored by Agreed, not for data stored by DocuSign or any other e-signature software.

Liability

The liability cap is equal to 12 months of fees.

Nothing Agreed does constitutes legal advice.
